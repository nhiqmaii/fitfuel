Questions? Good.

Where does my data live?

Entirely in your browser via localStorage. It never touches a server because there isn't one.

Do I need to create an account?

Nope. No email, no password, no "we value your privacy" cookie banner nonsense.

Does it work offline?

Yes! Once loaded, it's just static files. Toss your Wi-Fi in the trash if you like.
